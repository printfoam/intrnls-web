How we calculated it

A model of standard equipment, run on real weather.

We took the hourly weather NOAA recorded for your city from 2011 to 2024 and worked out, for each hour, how hard the fans of a standard tower or air cooler of your size would have to run. We added up a typical year, then multiplied by your power price.

For 2030 and 2050 we shifted that weather by the middle of eight climate models. The numbers describe standard equipment, not our media, and not your exact site.

A short note on the method is on our website.
