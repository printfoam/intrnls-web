What we do

Three ways in.

Most engagements move through all three, in order; a few start further along. None of them are small, and none of them stop at the prototype.

All capabilities →
01

Materials

New geometry needs new stuff to be made of. We develop and test the polymer composites a design will actually live in.

02

Design + prototyping

We design structures that do a physical job — moving heat, mass, load, or water — and build them at real scale, so the answer is a part in hand, not a slide.

03

Pilot → commercialization

A prototype that works once is not a product. We take the design into a live facility, learn what breaks, and carry it toward manufacturing at volume.
